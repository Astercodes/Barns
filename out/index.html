THE QUESTION BEHIND BARNS

Bigger harvests need bigger barns.

Most of us spend our lives pursuing more—opportunity, income, responsibility, influence, knowledge, impact, success.

If more came tomorrow,
could I carry it well?

Receiving more and having the capacity to sustain more are two different things. BARNS exists for the space between the two.

THE CAPACITY PROBLEM

ANCIENT WISDOM. MODERN CAPACITY BUILDING.

Prepare the container for the increase.

Joseph prepared storage before abundance. A widow gathered vessels before the oil multiplied. Isaiah spoke of enlarging the tent and strengthening its stakes. Jesus taught that faithfulness with little precedes responsibility for more.

These principles became the foundation of BARNS.

You don't have to share the Christian faith to use BARNS.

You only need to ask:
What would my life require of me if what I'm building actually became bigger?
